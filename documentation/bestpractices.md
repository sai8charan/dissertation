Use rules only if applicable these best practices
Strict Rules:

1. Follow a formal academic writing style.
2. Maintain an objective and neutral tone throughout.
3. Do NOT use first-person pronouns such as:
   - I
   - We
   - Our
   - Us
4. Do NOT use conversational expressions like:
   - "Let's discuss"
   - "As we know"
   - "In this article"
   - "You can see"
5. Write original content in your own words.
6. Avoid copying phrases commonly found on websites, papers, or textbooks.
7. Ensure low plagiarism by:
   - Paraphrasing concepts completely.
   - Using varied sentence structures.
   - Combining information from multiple perspectives.
   - Including analysis rather than merely describing facts.
8. Maintain logical flow between paragraphs.
9. Use consistent terminology throughout the document.
10. Explain technical terms when they first appear.
11. Avoid exaggerated claims such as:
    - "Best"
    - "Perfect"
    - "Revolutionary"
    - "Guaranteed"
12. Every paragraph should focus on a single idea and smoothly transition to the next.
13. Avoid unnecessary repetition.
14. Use proper grammar, punctuation, and spelling.
15. Keep sentences clear and concise.
16. Preserve the exact heading names provided.
17. Do not alter the numbering format of headings.
18. If references are needed, cite them in IEEE format.
19. Do not invent statistics, datasets, experiments, or references.
20. Ensure the final output is suitable for university/academic submission.

For each heading:
- Identify the key concepts to be covered.
- Present the concepts in a logical sequence.
- Include definitions, explanations, advantages, limitations, and examples where relevant.
- Add critical analysis instead of only descriptive text.
- Maintain coherence with previous and subsequent sections.

Output only the content for the requested headings.
